HOW TO PLAY

Controls & Rules

1. Serving

When it's your turn to serve, pull paddle down and swipe upward across the ball to launch across the net.

2. Aiming & Returning

Glide your paddle to intercept the ball after it bounces on your court. Swipe angle and speed direct ball placement.

3. Topspin & Smashes

Fast upward strokes generate heavy topspin with a sharp table dip. High-speed swings unleash Smash Winners!

4. Rules & Scoring

First to 11 points wins (must win by 2). Service alternates every 2 points. At 10-10 Deuce, serve alternates every point.
